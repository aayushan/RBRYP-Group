Comforting flavours, warm ambiance, and memorable family dining.

The Dining Experience

Fresh, Comforting Flavours For the Whole Family

The Family Bawarchi Restaurant balances a rich variety of cuisines with pure comfort. We serve a relaxed buffet breakfast starting at 8:00 AM and keep our kitchen active until 10:30 PM with a fresh à la carte menu.

Seating 44 guests, our dining space is thoughtfully arranged to remain intimate, making it the perfect setting for family dinners, cozy gatherings, and celebratory meals.
