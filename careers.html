Build Your Career With Us

Join a family-run group with seven decades of trust. We're always looking for committed, talented people to grow with us across jewellery, automobiles, hospitality and education.

Why Build a Career Here

Grow With a Group That Cares

Real Growth

Across four divisions and more than ten brands, there's always room to learn, take on more, and rise — many of our leaders grew from within.

Family Culture

We treat our team like family — with respect, fairness, and genuine care for your wellbeing and your family's.

Learn & Train

Work alongside global brands like Kia, KTM, Bajaj and Kalyan Jewellers, with on-the-job training and skill development.

Recognition

Hard work is seen and rewarded. We celebrate performance, loyalty, and the people who make our group what it is.

Stability

A 70-year-old, financially sound, family-owned group offers a secure, dependable place to build a long-term career.

Community Impact

Be part of a group deeply rooted in Motihari — your work supports education, livelihoods, and the wider community.

Life at RBRYP

More Than a Job
