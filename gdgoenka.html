Student Care

Pastoral Care & On-Site Boarding House

A caring residential and pastoral environment that supports students academically, socially, emotionally and physically.

Pastoral Care

  • Emotional well-being — helping students manage stress, worries and challenges.
  • Mentoring & guidance — teachers act as mentors for both personal and academic growth.
  • Student safety — a secure and nurturing environment, always.
  • Relationships — encouraging respect, empathy and cooperation.
  • Personal development — building confidence, resilience and leadership.
  • Inclusion & belonging — every child is a valued part of the community.
  • Early support — spotting when a student needs help, and connecting them with it.
