A Legacy of Trust & Excellence

Founded by the vision of its founders, Ram Bhanwan Ram Yamuna Prasad Group has grown from a humble beginning into one of the region's most respected multi-sector business houses. Our journey is built on integrity, quality, and an unwavering commitment to our customers.

From the sparkle of our jewellery to the excellence of our educational institutions — every venture we undertake is driven by a single promise: to deliver the best to our community.
