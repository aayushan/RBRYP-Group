Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

1956

The Humble Beginning

Shri Rambhawan Ram laid the foundation of the group with a simple philosophy: work diligently, earn trust, and grow with society.

56
70s
Generation II

Expanding the Vision

Shri Yamuna Prasad took the legacy to new heights by expanding into the jewellery segment, giving the group a broader identity.

Generation III

Diversification

Shri Krishna Kumar diversified the group into Automobiles and established GD Goenka Public School & the Educational Trust.

90s
Now
Generation IV

Modern Era

Led by Shri Amit Kumar and Shri Sumit Kumar, the group expanded with Yamuna Prasad Krishna Kumar Jewellers, Kia, KTM, and Bajaj.
