About the Brand

The Heart of Our Jewellery Legacy

A trusted regional jewellery brand offering gold, diamond, silver, bridal and gifting collections with warm, personalised service.

Every ornament reflects the purity, artistry and personal care that have earned the trust of families across the region for decades. Our showroom trades under the name Yamuna Prasad Krishna Kumar Jewellers, with YKJ Swarna Sarovar as our online outlet for browsing and enquiries.
