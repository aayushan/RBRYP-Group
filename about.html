Our Story & Legacy

Discover the journey, values, and visionary leadership that have made RBRYP Group a trusted name for over seven decades.

Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Values

In 1956, Shri Rambhawan Ram laid the foundation of the Ram Bhanwan Ram Yamuna Prasad Group with a humble beginning. His philosophy was simple – work diligently, earn trust, and grow together with society. Following him, his son Shri Yamuna Prasad took this legacy to new heights by expanding into the jewellery segment, giving the group a new identity and broader recognition.

Walking in the footsteps of his predecessors, Shri Krishna Kumar further diversified the group by establishing Rambhawan Ram Automobiles and the Rambhawan Ram Educational Trust (GD Goenka Public School). Through these institutions, thousands of young minds found the guidance and opportunities to pursue their dreams.

Today, this remarkable legacy is being carried forward by Shri Amit Kumar and Shri Sumit Kumar. Under their leadership, the group has expanded to include renowned brands such as Kalyan Jewellers, KTM, Kia, and Bajaj — transforming the Group into a trusted and respected multi-segment enterprise.
