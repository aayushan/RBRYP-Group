Growing Legacies

Legacy is about building the future by honouring the past. Founded in 1956 by Shri Rambhawan Ram, the Ram Bhawan Ram Yamuna Prasad Group has grown — across four generations — into a trusted, multi-sector enterprise spanning jewellery, automobiles, hospitality and education, rooted in the communities it serves.

⌄
Where It All Began

The Streets of Motihari

It started here — on the bustling lanes of Motihari, Bihar. Amid the bazaars, bullock carts and rickshaws of a growing town, Shri Rambhawan Ram opened a modest shop in 1956 with nothing more than diligence and the trust of his neighbours.

These streets shaped our values and our name. Seven decades on, the same town that gave us our beginning remains the heart of everything the Group does.

Who We Are

Built on Trust, Driven by Values

In 1956, Shri Rambhawan Ram laid the foundation of the RBRYP Group with a simple philosophy — work diligently, earn trust, and grow together with society. Four generations later, that promise has blossomed into 10 enterprises spanning jewellery, automobiles, hospitality, and education.

Today, Shri Amit Kumar and Shri Sumit Kumar carry this legacy forward, adding global brands like Yamuna Prasad Krishna Kumar Jewellers, Kia, KTM, and Triumph to the group's proud portfolio.

Our Story of Service

Rooted in the Community, Growing Together

When Shri Rambhawan Ram started with a humble shop in 1956, he made a quiet promise — that every rupee earned would carry the responsibility of giving back. That promise has been honoured across four generations.

From funding a school that today educates thousands, to stepping in during floods with relief supplies, the RBRYP Group has never treated community service as a line item — it is our founding purpose.

"Grow with society, not above it."
— Shri Rambhawan Ram, Founder
